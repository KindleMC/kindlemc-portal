# ⚖️ Statutory Laws & Server Code
*Standard Operational Guidelines*

---

### Chapter 1: Property Protection
* **Logging & Safety:** Every block placement, break, and chest transaction is logged for 90 days via Ledger (`/ledger inspect`)[cite: 108, 145].
* **Griefing & Theft:** Taking from containers or modifying builds that aren't yours results in an interim ban and rollback (`/ledger rollback`)[cite: 108, 110, 145, 147].

---

### Chapter 2: Technical Duping Rules
* **Allowed Mechanics:** TNT, carpet, rail, and gravity block (sand/gravel) duplication are permitted for technical farms[cite: 23, 108, 145].
* **Banned Items:** Duping general items, armor, weapons, or shulkers is strictly prohibited[cite: 23, 108, 145].

---

### Chapter 3: Enactment Threshold
Statutory laws can be adjusted with a **50% vote by the Moderator Team**[cite: 137, 174].
