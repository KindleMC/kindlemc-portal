# ⚖️ Statutory Laws & Server Code
*All Specific Server Rules, Guidelines, and SOPs*

---

### Chapter 1: Constitutional Subordination
* **Source of Authority:** Every statutory law derives its legitimacy from the Constitution.
* **Validity Check:** Any statute or staff guideline found to violate constitutional guarantees is void and unenforceable.

---

### Chapter 2: How to Join & Whitelist Procedure
1. **Read the Rules:** Carefully read through all statutory guidelines below.
2. **Submit Application:** Post your application message in the exact required format:
   `kindness [YourMinecraftUsername] [Today's Date]` *(Example: `kindness Steve 10/12/2026`)*
3. **Password Protection:** Never share the secret format or clue with others. Every player must read the rules individually.

---

### Chapter 3: Eligibility & Community Guidelines
* **13+ Age Requirement:** Players must be at least 13 years old (or the legal minimum age for Discord in their country).
* **Strict Age Privacy Policy:** Staff and players are strictly prohibited from asking anyone for their age, real name, location, or personal social media. Never share your exact birthdate. Staff will never ban players based on suspected age.
* **Community Advice:** Speak English in public channels, complete your schoolwork before playing, and keep your gaming gear clean!

---

### Chapter 4: The Five Core Conduct Rules
1. **No Stealing or Griefing:** Never modify builds or take items from containers that aren't yours—even if they appear abandoned. Every block break, placement, and chest transaction is logged for 90 days (`/ledger inspect`). Offenders face interim bans, inventory wipes, and block rollbacks (`/ledger rollback`).
2. **Protect Privacy:** Voice chat recording is strictly forbidden. Do not post screenshots or videos showing other players' nametags outside Kindle MC without explicit permission.
3. **No Exploiting or Unfair Clients:** X-ray, fullbright, seed cracking, hacked clients, and bots are banned.
4. **No Self-Promotion or Poaching:** Advertising private Discords, Realms, or other servers in chat, DMs, or voice calls is forbidden.
5. **Keep It Safe & SFW:** Hate speech, slurs, harassment, NSFW content, gore, and political or religious debates result in immediate enforcement.

---

### Chapter 5: Gameplay, Redstone & Duping Regulations
* **Consent-Based PvP:** Combat is allowed only when both participants explicitly agree beforehand. Unprovoked attacks or traps are treated as griefing.
* **Redstone & Lag Prevention:** Excessive automated farms or intentional lag machines are prohibited. Ask staff via a ticket before constructing large redstone projects or spawn builds (within x, z ±500).
* **Allowed Technical Duping:** TNT, carpet, rail, and gravity block (sand/gravel) duplication are permitted for technical farms.
* **Banned Item Duping:** Duplication of general items, gear, tools, armor, weapons, or shulker boxes is strictly banned.

---

### Chapter 6: Legislative Procedure & Staff SOP
* **Statutory Enactment:** Creating, modifying, or repealing any statutory rule requires a majority vote by members holding the **Legislative (`Leg`)** role.
* **Moderation Philosophy:** Rules exist to protect our wholesome sanctuary. Staff play survival without operator cheats and handle all moderation privately inside support tickets.
* **Interim Ban Protocol:** Serious violations trigger an immediate interim ban to secure the server. Staff collect proof via `/ledger inspect`, review the case in a ticket, and apply the appropriate timeout or unban.
* **Ban Hierarchy:** Moderators handle mutes, kicks, and temporary bans under 30 days. Bans lasting 1 month+ or Permanent Bans require a 7-day vote in `#court`.
